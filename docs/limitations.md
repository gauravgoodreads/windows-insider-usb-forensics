# Limitations

- The case is synthetic and laboratory-controlled; external validity is limited.
- Windows version, logging policy, filesystem, application, and acquisition choices change artifact availability.
- Timestamp semantics differ; missing or conflicting timestamps require manual examination.
- USB presence does not by itself establish transfer; correlation does not establish causation or intent.
- Deleted data may be unrecoverable and export tools can omit context.
- The demonstration inputs resemble normalized exports, not fabricated Registry/event-log evidence and not a substitute for a real acquisition.
- Classification, confidence, thresholds, and scoring are project-defined and not forensic standards.
- Binary E01/EVTX/Registry parsing and formal statistical validation are outside the implemented core.
